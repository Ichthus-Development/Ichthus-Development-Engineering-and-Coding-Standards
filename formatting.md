# Source Formatting Standards

> **Policy status:** Initial scope. Exact repository-wide values have not yet been adopted and must not be inferred from this outline.

Source must remain readable and intention-revealing without specialized tooling. Formatting automation should support that goal and must not obscure semantics.

Future revisions should deliberately establish requirements for encoding, line endings, final newlines, indentation, tabs and spaces, trailing whitespace, blank lines, line width, Markdown, generated files, and formatter configuration.

Language-specific formatting remains authoritative in its language document. See [.NET Standards](dotnet.md) and [SQL Formatting](sql.md#formatting-and-readability).
